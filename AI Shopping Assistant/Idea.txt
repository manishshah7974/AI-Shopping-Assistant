AI Shopping Assistant

What it does

Users ask:

“Show me Arabic perfumes for women under 40 KD with oud and vanilla notes”
“I want something like Tom Ford Oud Wood but cheaper”
“Gift perfume for wedding”
“Long-lasting spicy fragrance for winter”
“Best perfume for office use”

The assistant:

understands intent
retrieves products using RAG + vector search
ranks products intelligently
explains recommendations naturally
remembers session context

Why normal search fails

Elasticsearch keyword search cannot understand:

intent
fragrance similarity
occasion
mood
semantic meaning

LLM + vector retrieval solves this.
